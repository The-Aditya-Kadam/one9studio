FAQ

Common questions.

What size creators do you work with?

All sizes — from nano creators with highly engaged niche audiences to celebrity talent. The mix depends on your goals and budget.

How do you check a creator is genuine?

We review audience data, growth patterns, comment quality and past brand work before shortlisting anyone.

Who owns the content?

Usage rights are agreed in each contract. We can negotiate paid-ad and website usage as part of the deal.

What is the minimum budget?

Campaigns typically start from ₹2 lakh including creator fees, depending on scale and deliverables.
