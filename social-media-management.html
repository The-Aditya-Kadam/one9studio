FAQ

Common questions.

Which platforms do you manage?

Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, X, Facebook and Snapchat. We recommend focusing on the two or three that matter most for your audience.

Do we approve content before it goes live?

Yes. Every month’s calendar and every post is shared for approval before publishing.

Is ad spend included?

Our fee covers management; media spend is billed separately and goes directly to the platforms.

How quickly will we see results?

Engagement usually improves in the first month; meaningful follower and lead growth typically shows by month three.
