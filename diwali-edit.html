Buyer FAQ

Before you sign in.

Is the catalogue free to use?

Yes. Create a free account with your name, company, WhatsApp number and city to see pricing on all products. No credit card.

Do I order from Sampling Store or the supplier?

Sampling Store connects you with the supplier. Pricing, invoicing and fulfilment are handled directly with them.

Can I see samples physically?

Yes. Samples for every listed product are stocked at the Andheri East hub in Mumbai. Message us to book a visit.

Can products be customised with our logo?

Most hampers, stationery and décor items support logo printing or engraving. Customisation options and extra lead time are listed per product.

What if I need something not in the catalogue?

Send a brief on WhatsApp. With 23 suppliers across categories, we can usually source it or suggest an alternative within a day.
