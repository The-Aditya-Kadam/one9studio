FAQ

Common questions.

Do you work with early-stage brands?

Yes. We run a lighter foundation package for new brands and scale up as you grow.

How long does a rebrand take?

Most brand strategy and identity projects take 6–10 weeks, depending on scope and approvals.

Can you work with our in-house team?

Absolutely. We often act as the strategic lead while your team handles day-to-day execution.

How do you measure brand growth?

We track awareness, sentiment, branded search, share of voice and sales indicators agreed at kickoff.
