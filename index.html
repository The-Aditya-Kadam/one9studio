About us

A shared warehouse for the gifting industry.

Sampling Store is a centralized hub where brands and manufacturers can stock their product samples. Instead of individually sending or storing samples, they deposit them at the Sampling Store, which acts as a shared warehouse for every corporate buyer in the network.

Headquartered in Andheri East, Mumbai, run by importers who know what the gifting season actually needs.
