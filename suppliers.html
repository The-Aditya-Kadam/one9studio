Supplier FAQ

Questions importers ask us.

Who can list on Sampling Store?

Importers, manufacturers and brand owners with products suited to corporate gifting: hampers, dry fruits, sweets, home décor, wellness, stationery, tech accessories and similar.

Do I lose my samples?

No. Samples stay your property at the hub. You can swap, top up or withdraw them with notice.

Who handles the buyer conversation?

You do. We qualify and route the enquiry; pricing, negotiation and fulfilment stay between you and the buyer.

Is pricing shown publicly?

Prices are visible only to buyers who create a free account, so casual browsers never see your price list.

How fast can I be listed for Diwali?

With samples and a price list ready, listings typically go live within a few working days of the review call.
